About

Pickleball deserves serious coverage.

Pickleball Cosmos is an independent publication about the rules, equipment, data, history, culture and business of the sport.

The aim is straightforward: explain the game clearly, report from strong sources and build original reference material worth returning to.

Independent by design

Pickleball Cosmos is built as an institution-first publication rather than a founder personality brand. We use the Pickleball Cosmos Editorial byline for publication-level work and do not publish individual contributor identities as part of the editorial product.

That privacy choice does not reduce the standard of accountability. It increases the importance of what readers can inspect directly: primary sources, governing records, calculation methods, evidence labels, update dates, testing disclosures and corrections.

What we publish

Learn covers rules, scoring and the mechanics of the game. Gear examines specifications, approval records, buying decisions and, when available, documented testing. Data tracks participation, infrastructure, rankings and the economics of the sport. Stories covers history, people, culture and developments that merit deeper treatment.

What we will not do

We do not invent expert personas to compensate for private contributor identities. We do not claim hands-on testing that did not occur. We do not turn estimates into measured facts or fill gaps in historical series without labeling the calculation. Press releases are source material, not finished reporting. Confidence is not a substitute for evidence.

How accountability works

Work published under the Pickleball Cosmos Editorial byline is governed by a common standard for sourcing, attribution, uncertainty, commercial independence and corrections. For consequential claims, readers should be able to trace the evidence trail rather than rely on an unverifiable personal credential.

Our methodology explains how rules, data and gear evidence are handled. Our corrections policy explains what happens when a material fact is wrong or changes after publication.

Corrections and change

Rules change, paddle approvals change, manufacturers revise specifications, rankings move and datasets are updated. Accuracy is therefore not a one-time publication event. Where a material change affects the answer, the relevant page should be revised and dated.
