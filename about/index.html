About

Pickleball without the noise.

Pickleball Cosmos is an independent publication built around clear explanations, useful gear guidance, original data and stories worth reading.

Our aim is simple: make pickleball easier to understand and more interesting to follow. That means practical rule guides, thoughtful equipment coverage, data-led features and selective reporting rather than endless filler.

Editorial approach

We distinguish between facts, analysis and opinion. When an article relies on official rules, participation data or manufacturer specifications, we aim to identify the source clearly.

Gear coverage

We do not claim hands-on testing unless we have actually tested a product. Early gear guides may compare published specifications, approval status, reputable third-party measurements and buyer feedback. Our methodology will become more testing-led as the publication grows.

Cosmos principles

Clear writing. Useful information. No fake testing. No filler.
