About

Pickleball deserves serious coverage.

Pickleball Cosmos is being built as an independent sports-media publication for a sport that has outgrown novelty coverage.

Our scope is deliberately broad: rules, equipment, participation data, history, culture and the business of the sport. The common thread is editorial discipline.

What we are trying to build

A publication that feels useful to a first-time player, credible to an experienced player and worth citing for someone following pickleball as a fast-growing sports market.

What we will not do

We will not publish fake hands-on reviews. We will not smooth incomplete historical datasets into invented numbers. We will not turn every press release into “news.” And we will not confuse confidence with evidence.

Who is responsible for the work?

Articles published under the Pickleball Cosmos Editorial byline are publication-level work researched, checked and published under the site's editorial standards. We use that organizational byline instead of inventing named contributors who did not actually report the piece.

Corrections and accountability

Rules change, paddle approvals change, manufacturers update specifications and better evidence can replace earlier estimates. Our corrections policy explains how material factual errors and editorial updates are handled.

Editorial principle: if a primary source exists, use it. If the evidence is incomplete, say so.
