PPA World Pickleball Rankings

The PPA World Pickleball Ranking in this snapshot is an overall measure across three disciplines: a player's 14 best PPA results in the trailing 52 weeks, weighted 50% gender doubles, 35% mixed doubles and 15% singles. It is distinct from PPA's discipline-specific live tables; see the source record below before comparing the two.
