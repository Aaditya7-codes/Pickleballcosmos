Pickleball World Rankings

Current professional rankings, with unlike ranking systems kept separate and the underlying snapshots preserved. The goal is a historical record of who ranked where, when, and under which methodology — not another copied leaderboard.

By Pickleball Cosmos EditorialSnapshot: August 13, 2026
There is no single universal ranking. PPA introduced a composite World Pickleball Ranking on August 5, 2026. The Global Pickleball Alliance runs a separate 12-month system by discipline. Pickleball Cosmos reports each system on its own terms rather than averaging unlike methodologies.

Why the systems disagree

PPA World RankingGPA Rankings
StructureComposite overall rankingSeparate discipline rankings
WindowTrailing 52 weeksCurrent 12 months

Cosmos policy: official rankings are attributed to the organization that produced them. We do not invent missing observations, silently overwrite historical values, or combine incompatible systems into a made-up consensus table.
