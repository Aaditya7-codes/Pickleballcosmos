Cosmos Gear Intelligence

A structured evidence layer for pickleball equipment. We track what is known about a paddle, where each claim came from and how much confidence the record deserves.

Pickleball Cosmos EditorialLaunched August 16, 2026
What this is: governing records, manufacturer specifications, dated prices, warranty terms and attributed independent measurements organized into a comparable evidence record. What it is not: a claim that Pickleball Cosmos physically tested a paddle.

Why this exists

Pickleball equipment data is fragmented. Approval status may sit in a governing database, dimensions and construction on a manufacturer page, warranty language somewhere else, and swing-weight or twist-weight measurements with specialist testers using their own methods and samples.

Gear Intelligence keeps those evidence types separate rather than blending them into one unsupported score. The objective is not to manufacture certainty. It is to make comparison easier without losing provenance.

The evidence stack

Evidence typeWhat Cosmos does with itWhat it does not become
Governing recordsTrack approval, certification and compliance status for the exact model where the record supports it.A performance endorsement.
Manufacturer specificationsRecord dimensions, stated weight, construction, materials, price and warranty with the manufacturer kept as the source.An independent measurement.
Independent measurementsPreserve tester attribution, sample context and method where available; compare ranges only when the measurements are meaningfully comparable.A Pickleball Cosmos measurement.
Cosmos calculationsNormalize units, compare prices and warranties, derive transparent ratios or ranges and identify contradictions in the source record.A hidden composite rating.
Hands-on testingReserved for a future Gear Lab using physical samples and a documented protocol.Something implied by desk research.

No single “Cosmos Score”

A 94/100 badge can create false precision when the underlying evidence comes from different sources, samples and methods. Gear Intelligence instead exposes the dimensions that matter: approval evidence, specifications, measured evidence, warranty, price, freshness and evidence confidence.

Editorial recommendations may still be made. The reasoning has to remain visible.

Evidence confidence

LabelMeaning
HighThe exact or clearly matched model has strong first-party specification evidence plus a governing record or comparably strong verification.
MediumThe record is useful but one important element depends on a manufacturer statement, partial model mapping or incomplete independent measurement coverage.
LimitedAn exact-model match, approval conclusion or another material field still needs stronger verification.

Confidence describes the evidence record, not whether a paddle is good or bad.

What we will track as the database expands

  • Exact brand, model, generation and variant identity.
  • Current approval/compliance record and date checked.
  • Manufacturer dimensions, weight range, shape, thickness, materials and construction claims.
  • Current price, price-check date and availability.
  • Warranty duration, registration requirements and material exclusions.
  • Attributed independent swing-weight, twist-weight, spin, power or other measurements where methods and provenance are usable.
  • Source conflicts, model-code ambiguity and changes over time.

Standards

Gear Intelligence follows the Pickleball Cosmos editorial and gear methodology. Approval status, prices, warranties and product specifications can change; dated records should be read as observations from the stated check date.

Evidence rule

Manufacturer claims stay manufacturer claims. External measurements stay attributed to their tester. “Tested by Cosmos” is reserved for actual physical testing.
