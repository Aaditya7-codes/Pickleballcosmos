What the first 50 records already show

Approval has to be variant-specific.

Paddletek's current Honeyfoam pages explicitly distinguish 14mm variants that are UPA-approved but exceed the power threshold for USA Pickleball-sanctioned events. Brand-level “approved” language is therefore not enough.

Published measurements are not all independent measurements.

Selkirk, Six Zero, Volair, Franklin and some other manufacturers publish swing-weight or twist-weight figures for selected models. Gear Intelligence stores those numbers as manufacturer data. They do not become Pickleball Cosmos measurements.

Availability is part of the evidence.

Some still-relevant models are currently shown as unavailable by their manufacturer. The database records that state instead of silently treating every model as purchasable.

What each record can contain

  • Exact brand, model, shape and thickness.
  • Current price observation, MSRP where available, date checked and availability state.
  • USA Pickleball evidence and direct governing-record link where we have an exact match.
  • UPA status when the manufacturer or governing record supports it.
  • Length, width, handle, grip, manufacturer weight and construction fields.
  • Manufacturer-published swing weight and twist weight, explicitly kept separate from independent or Cosmos measurements.
  • Warranty terms, evidence confidence, source links and verification notes.
No single Cosmos Score: a 94/100 badge would imply a level of comparability the source record often does not justify. Gear Intelligence exposes the dimensions instead. Editorial recommendations can still be made, but the reasoning has to remain visible.

Evidence rule
