How to Choose Your First Pickleball Paddle

A beginner-friendly framework for choosing a pickleball paddle without getting lost in specs.

Published August 2026 · Pickleball Cosmos

Your first paddle does not need to be exotic. It needs to be comfortable, predictable and good enough that you can learn proper technique.

Start with weight

A midweight paddle is the safest starting point for most beginners. Extremely heavy paddles can fatigue the arm; extremely light paddles may feel unstable.

Then consider shape

Standard and hybrid shapes generally offer an accessible balance of reach, sweet spot and hand speed. Elongated paddles can add reach but may feel less forgiving.

Thickness and feel

A 16mm paddle is a common beginner-friendly choice because it usually emphasizes control and stability.

Do not buy on marketing language alone

Terms such as “pro,” “power,” “carbon” and “spin” can describe real features, but they do not automatically make a paddle better for you. Focus on the complete specification and the type of game you are trying to develop.
