14mm vs 16mm Pickleball Paddles

The practical difference between 14mm and 16mm pickleball paddles, and which thickness is likely to suit your game.

Published August 2026 · Pickleball Cosmos

Paddle thickness affects feel, stability and how quickly the ball leaves the face. The difference between 14mm and 16mm is small on paper but noticeable on court.

Why players choose 14mm

Thinner paddles often feel faster through the air and can produce a more direct, lively response. Players who value hand speed and a crisp feel may prefer them.

Why players choose 16mm

Thicker paddles are often associated with a softer feel, greater stability and easier control on resets and dinks. Many intermediate players gravitate toward 16mm for that reason.

Which should a beginner choose?

There is no universal answer, but a forgiving 16mm paddle is often an easier starting point for a player still developing control. Shape, weight, swing weight and face material matter too, so thickness should never be the only buying criterion.

Cosmos rule of thumb: prioritize control and forgiveness first; chase speed and pop later.
