14mm vs 16mm Pickleball Paddles

A practical explanation of how paddle thickness affects feel, stability and control.

By Pickleball Cosmos EditorialUpdated August 10, 2026

Paddle thickness is one of the most visible specifications in modern pickleball, but it should not be treated as a performance score. Shape, total weight, swing weight, twist weight, face construction and core design all influence how a paddle behaves.

What 14mm commonly changes

Thinner paddles often produce a more direct, lively response and may feel faster in hand, though construction can override the generalization.

What 16mm commonly changes

Thicker paddles are often associated with a softer response, greater stability and easier control on dinks and resets.

Which is easier for a beginner?

A forgiving 16mm paddle is often a sensible starting point, but thickness alone should not drive the purchase. A well-balanced 14mm design can be easier to use than an unstable or poorly matched 16mm paddle.
