Wrong Score Called in Pickleball: Can You Stop the Rally?

Yes — but only within a narrow window. Under the 2026 USA Pickleball rule, a player may stop the rally to challenge the score before the return of serve is hit and before the ball becomes dead.

By Pickleball Cosmos EditorialChecked August 13, 2026
The ruling: if the called score is wrong and you stop in time, replay the rally with the correct score. If the score was actually correct, stopping is a fault. If you wait until after the return of serve is hit, stopping is also a fault.

The 2026 decision table

What happened?Ruling
Wrong score called; player stops before the return of serve and before the ball is deadReplay with the correct score.
Correct score called; player stops the rally to challenge itFault on the player who stopped play.
Player stops after the return of serve is hitFault on the player who stopped play.
Wrong score called but rally is completedThe rally result stands; correct the score before the next serve.

Why “before the return” is not the whole rule

The 2026 wording requires both conditions to remain true: the return of serve has not been hit and the ball has not become dead. That matters if the serve itself ends the rally. Once the ball is dead, the rally is in the books even if no return was hit.

Three examples

Wrong and timely: the server calls 5-3-2, but the correct score is 5-3-1. The receiver stops play before returning the live serve. Correct the score and replay.

Correct score challenged: a player stops the rally because they mistakenly think the score was wrong. The stoppage is a fault on that player.

Too late: the serve is returned and only then does a player stop the rally to question the score. That is a late challenge and a fault. If nobody stops play and the rally finishes, the result stands and the score is corrected before the next serve.

Different from the wrong server or receiver

A wrong score call concerns the score announced before the serve. A wrong server, receiver or position concerns who should be serving, receiving or standing where. The remedies are not interchangeable.

What changed for 2026?

USA Pickleball approved a clarification defining when a wrong-score challenge produces a replay and when stopping play produces a fault. In the reorganized 2026 rulebook, the provision appears under Rule 6.F, “Challenging the Score Call.”

Quick rule

Wrong + timely: replay.
Correct score challenged: fault.
Late stop: fault.
Rally completed: result stands.
