Pickleball Court Dimensions Explained

Pickleball court size, kitchen dimensions and practical layout explained in plain English.

Published August 2026 · Pickleball Cosmos

A standard pickleball court is 20 feet wide and 44 feet long. The same playing dimensions are used for singles and doubles.

The non-volley zone

The kitchen extends 7 feet from the net on each side. That makes the non-volley zone 14 feet deep in total across both sides of the net.

Why the compact court changes the sport

Because players are much closer together than in tennis, reaction speed, placement and control become unusually important. The dimensions are a major reason pickleball produces fast exchanges at the net.
