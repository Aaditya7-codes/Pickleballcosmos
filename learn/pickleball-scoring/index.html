How Pickleball Scoring Works

Pickleball scoring explained simply, including doubles score calls such as 0-0-2.

Published August 2026 · Pickleball Cosmos

Pickleball scoring can sound strange at first, especially in doubles. The easiest way to understand it is to separate the team score from the server number.

Doubles uses three numbers

A call such as 4-2-1 means the serving team has 4 points, the receiving team has 2 points, and the first server for that team is serving.

Why games often begin 0-0-2

At the beginning of a traditional doubles game, the opening serving team gets only one service turn before a side-out. Calling 0-0-2 reflects that special opening condition.

Winning the game

Many recreational games are played to 11 and must be won by two points. Tournament formats may vary, so always confirm the format being used.
