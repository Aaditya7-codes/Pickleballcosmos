Pickleball Scoring: Why 0–0–2 Makes Sense

Doubles scoring explained in plain English, including the three-number score call.

By Pickleball Cosmos EditorialUpdated August 10, 2026

Doubles scoring sounds complicated because the score call contains three numbers. Once you know what each number represents, the system becomes much easier.

The three numbers

A score such as 4–2–1 means the serving team has 4 points, the receiving team has 2 points, and the first server for that team is serving.

Why games can begin 0–0–2

The opening serving team starts with only one service turn before the first side-out. Calling 0–0–2 represents that special opening state within traditional side-out scoring.

Winning

Many recreational games are played to 11 and require a two-point margin. Tournament formats can vary, so the event format should always be checked.
