What Is the Kitchen in Pickleball?

A simple explanation of the pickleball kitchen, the non-volley zone and the mistakes beginners make most often.

Published August 2026 · Pickleball Cosmos

The “kitchen” is the nickname for the non-volley zone: the 7-foot area on both sides of the net.

The rule in one sentence

You cannot volley the ball while standing in the kitchen or touching its line.

Can you enter the kitchen?

Yes. You may step into the kitchen at any time to play a ball that has bounced. The restriction applies to volleys, not ordinary groundstrokes after a bounce.

Momentum matters

If you volley from outside the kitchen and your momentum carries you into it, that is still a fault. The rally is not saved merely because you contacted the ball from a legal position.

Easy memory rule: kitchen + volley = fault. Kitchen + bounce = usually fine.
