# Pickleball Cosmos Gear Lab — Benchmark Paddle Set v0.1

Purpose: choose the first physical paddles deliberately, so the lab learns across construction, shape, thickness, mass distribution, price and market relevance rather than buying random review units.

Status: acquisition shortlist, not tested.

## Selection principles

The first set should include:
- at least one affordable reference paddle;
- standard/widebody, hybrid and elongated shapes;
- 12–14 mm and 16 mm constructions;
- conventional polypropylene/honeycomb, thermoformed/foam-edge, full/structural foam and non-honeycomb construction;
- current mainstream brands plus specialist brands;
- paired variants where changing one design variable is especially useful;
- models with official/manufacturer specs that can be compared against Cosmos measurements.

## Priority A — first six purchases

1. **Vatic Pro PRISM Flash 16 mm**
   - Role: affordable control/reference paddle; raw T700 carbon; non-thermoformed positioning.
   - Why: establishes a lower-price benchmark and a well-known conventional construction reference.
   - Manufacturer source: Vatic Pro PRISM product page; current at research date.

2. **Six Zero Double Black Diamond Control 16 mm**
   - Role: mainstream thermoformed/all-court reference.
   - Why: useful comparison against PRISM for construction and mass-distribution effects at similar nominal thickness.
   - Manufacturer currently sells 16 mm, 14 mm and elongated variants.

3. **Franklin FS Tour Tempo 16 mm**
   - Role: traditional/wider shape reference.
   - Why: manufacturer publishes static weight, swing weight and twist weight, giving us a direct external check against Cosmos measurements.
   - Manufacturer published figures: 8.6–8.9 oz, swing weight 122 kg/cm², twist weight 7.84 kg/cm².

4. **Franklin FS Tour Dynasty 16 mm**
   - Role: elongated counterpart to Tempo 16.
   - Why: same product family and core concept with different geometry; strong controlled comparison for shape/mass distribution.
   - Manufacturer published figures: 8.5–8.8 oz, swing weight 134 kg/cm², twist weight 7.15 kg/cm².

5. **Paddletek Bantam TKO-CX 14.3 mm**
   - Role: elongated power reference.
   - Why: Paddletek publishes static-weight, swing-weight and twist-weight ranges, creating another validation target for our apparatus.
   - Current manufacturer range: 7.7–8.1 oz; swing weight 119–126; twist weight 6.6–7.2 for the TKO-CX family page.

6. **Gearbox Pro Ultimate Elongated 16 mm**
   - Role: non-honeycomb carbon-rib construction reference.
   - Why: materially different core architecture from conventional polypropylene/foam designs; tests whether our metrics illuminate construction differences without relying on marketing language.

## Priority B — next six

7. **Selkirk LABS Project 008, 13 mm or 16 mm**
   - Role: high-density foam-core reference.
   - Why: Selkirk positions Project 008 around PureFoam rather than polypropylene; useful for durability and structural comparisons later.

8. **CRBN³ TruFoam Barrage, 14 mm**
   - Role: current full/foam architecture, elongated.
   - Why: broadens foam-core sample beyond Selkirk and provides a current premium comparison.

9. **Engage Pursuit Pro1 Hybrid 12.7 mm**
   - Role: thin hybrid polymer-core reference.
   - Why: adds a US-made/engineered specialist construction and a different balance/shape strategy.

10. **Six Zero Double Black Diamond Control 14 mm**
    - Role: paired thickness test against the 16 mm DBD.
    - Why: same family lets us investigate how actual mass, balance and inertia move when nominal thickness changes.

11. **Vatic Pro PRISM Flash 14 mm**
    - Role: second paired-thickness control.
    - Why: provides a non-thermoformed 14-vs-16 comparison distinct from Six Zero.

12. **Franklin FS Tour Dynasty 14 mm**
    - Role: paired elongated 14-vs-16 reference.
    - Why: Franklin publishes manufacturer inertia numbers for both, making this a useful apparatus-validation and thickness/geometry case.

## Why this set is better than buying only flagship paddles

A Gear Lab needs reference diversity more than hype coverage. The first 12 are designed to create controlled comparisons:
- PRISM 14 vs 16;
- DBD 14 vs 16;
- Franklin Dynasty 14 vs 16;
- Franklin Tempo 16 vs Dynasty 16;
- conventional vs thermoformed vs foam vs carbon-rib core architecture;
- wider/traditional vs hybrid vs elongated geometry;
- roughly $100 entry reference through $250+ premium products.

Those comparisons will help validate whether our methodology produces sensible, repeatable distinctions before we scale to hundreds of paddles.

## Acquisition rules

- Prefer ordinary retail purchase for baseline samples.
- If a manufacturer supplies a sample, mark acquisition source and keep the identical protocol.
- Do not accept pre-selected 'golden samples' as the sole sample for high-impact comparative testing if retail purchase is practical.
- Retain tested paddles where possible for remeasurement and durability checks.
- Log purchase invoice, SKU/variant and serial/lot information when available.

## Before purchase

Re-check current USAP approval status, exact variant availability and model/version name immediately before ordering. Product lines change quickly, and an older variant must not be substituted without updating this document and database record.
