Pickleball Cosmos Editorial

This byline is used when responsibility for an article sits with the publication rather than a named contributor.

Editorial responsibility

Articles carrying the Pickleball Cosmos Editorial byline are published under a common standard for sourcing, attribution, uncertainty and corrections. The byline identifies the responsible publishing organization; it is not a claim of individual authorship.

Rules

Rules coverage starts with the current official rulebook and governing-body material. We separate the operative rule from shorthand, recreational convention and proposed future changes.

Gear

Gear coverage distinguishes manufacturer specifications, governing-body approval records, independent measurements and hands-on testing. Those categories are labeled rather than blended together. We do not describe a product as tested by Pickleball Cosmos unless we actually used or measured that physical product.

Data

Primary research publishers, governing bodies and first-party records are preferred where available. Estimates and calculations are identified as such. Missing observations are not presented as measured facts.

Commercial independence

Affiliate relationships, sponsorships and manufacturer access do not determine editorial conclusions. Relevant commercial relationships are disclosed where they could affect a reader's assessment of the work.

Corrections

Material factual errors are corrected. Rules, approval status, warranty terms and datasets can also change after publication; when that affects an article, the page should be updated and dated accordingly.
