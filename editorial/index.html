Pickleball Cosmos Editorial

Pickleball Cosmos Editorial is the publication-level author used when an article is researched, checked and published under the site's editorial process rather than credited to a named contributor.

What this byline means

It does not mean an anonymous source or an automated author. It means responsibility sits with Pickleball Cosmos as the publishing organization. Articles carrying this byline are expected to meet the same standards on sourcing, attribution, uncertainty and corrections.

Rules coverage

Rules articles prioritize the current official rulebook and governing-body materials. We distinguish the rule itself from common shorthand, recreational convention and proposed future changes.

Gear coverage

We separate manufacturer specifications, governing-body approval records, independent measurements, buyer feedback and hands-on testing. We do not describe a paddle as tested by Pickleball Cosmos unless the publication actually used or measured that physical product.

Data and research

Primary research publishers, governing bodies and first-party records are preferred where available. Estimates are labeled as estimates. Missing historical observations are not silently interpolated and presented as measured facts.

Commercial independence

Affiliate relationships, sponsorships and commercial access do not determine editorial conclusions. Where a commercial relationship is relevant to a reader's evaluation of coverage, it should be disclosed.

Accountability: the publication's standards, corrections approach and sourcing rules are public so readers can evaluate not only what we say, but how we reached it.
