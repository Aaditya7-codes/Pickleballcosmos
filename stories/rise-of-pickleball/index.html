The Rise of Pickleball: From Backyard Game to 24 Million Players

How pickleball evolved from a 1965 backyard game into a major American participation sport.

Published August 2026 · Pickleball Cosmos

Pickleball began in 1965 on Bainbridge Island, Washington. For decades, it remained a niche recreational activity. Then the curve changed.

From invention to national reach

The sport slowly developed organized rules, dedicated courts and national associations. By 1990, pickleball was being played in all 50 U.S. states.

The modern acceleration

The most dramatic growth came in the 2020s. Participation rose from roughly 4.2 million U.S. players in 2020 to 24.3 million in 2025, according to SFIA reporting.

Why the format travelled so well

Pickleball combines a short learning curve, compact court, social doubles format and relatively low barrier to entry. Those characteristics allowed it to spread quickly through parks, clubs, private facilities and converted tennis courts.

24.3 million U.S. participants in 2025 turned pickleball from a curiosity into a mainstream sports-business story.
